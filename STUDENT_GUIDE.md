# Student Guide: Account Activation & Live Classroom Participation
### Class Polling App — Live Classroom Portal

**Live Portal URL:** [https://polling-app-murex.vercel.app](https://polling-app-murex.vercel.app)

---

## 1. Who Can Participate? (Eligible Student Roster)

Access to **Class Polling App** is restricted to the **45 eligible students** enrolled in the course:
- **`241AI001` through `241AI044`** (44 students)
- **`231AI007`** (1 student)

Any Student ID outside this exact roster (for example, `241AI045` or an unapproved roll number) is automatically rejected by the server.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step: First-Time Student Account Activation

Before logging in for the first time, you must **activate** your eligible Student ID and set your personal password:

1. Open [https://polling-app-murex.vercel.app/student/activate](https://polling-app-murex.vercel.app/student/activate) on your phone or laptop (or click **"Activate Student ID"** from the login screen).
2. **Enter Your Student ID:**
   - Type your official roll number (e.g., `241AI001` or `231AI007`). It is case-insensitive (`241ai001` is automatically formatted to `241AI001`).
3. **Create a Password:**
   - Enter a personal password of **at least 6 characters** and confirm it.
4. Click **"Complete Activation"**:
   - The system verifies that your Student ID is on the 45-student eligible roster and has not already been activated.
   - Your account is immediately created, logged in, and taken directly to the **Join Classroom Session** screen.

> **What if my Student ID says "already activated" or I forgot my password?**
> Ask the Administrator or Teaching Assistant to click **"Reset Activation"** next to your Student ID in the Admin Console (`/admin`). Once reset, you can immediately re-activate your Student ID at `/student/activate` with a new password.

---

## 3. Returning Student Login

After you have activated your account once, you do **not** need to activate again for future lectures:

1. Visit [https://polling-app-murex.vercel.app/student/login](https://polling-app-murex.vercel.app/student/login).
2. Enter your **Student ID** (e.g., `241AI001`) and the **password** you created during activation.
3. Click **"Enter Student Portal"**.

---

## 4. Joining & Participating in a Live Classroom Session

1. **Join the Session:**
   - Either scan the **QR Code** projected by the professor (which automatically fills in the 6-character code) or enter the **6-character Class Join Code** (e.g., `9SMJZL`) and click **"Enter Classroom"**.
2. **Switching Between Lecture Questions:**
   - If the session has multiple questions, use the **Lecture Questions (`Q1`, `Q2`, `Q3`, ...)** bar at the top of your screen, or simply stay on the active question—your screen updates automatically in real time as the professor opens each round.
3. **Round 1 — Submit Your Initial Response:**
   - Provide your **Answer** (Multiple Choice selection, Numerical Estimate, or Free-Form Text depending on the question).
   - Select your **Confidence Rating (1 to 5)**.
   - Write a clear **Explanation / Reasoning** for why you believe your answer is right.
   - Click **"Submit Round 1 Response"**.
4. **Round 2 — Challenge & Revision Round:**
   - After the class submits Round 1, the professor releases a targeted **Round 2 Challenge** (a counterexample, edge case, or opposing argument).
   - Read the challenge carefully and decide whether to **keep your initial answer** or **revise your answer**, update your confidence rating, and briefly explain why the challenge did or did not change your mind.
5. **Personal Review & Instructor Benchmark:**
   - Once the professor completes the question, your screen displays your **Personal Submission & Performance Review**, comparing:
     - Your **Round 1 Initial Answer & Rationale**
     - The **Round 2 Challenge Prompt**
     - Your **Round 2 Revised Response & Justification**
     - The **Instructor Benchmark** (the professor's reference answer and expected conceptual reasoning)
